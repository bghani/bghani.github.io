I launched [Kaetij](https://kaetij.com/), an interactive web application for exploring the soundscape of Kashmir through animal sounds from Xeno-canto. The platform combines a geographic view with an acoustic similarity space, allowing users to listen to recordings and explore relationships between sounds across both space and acoustic structure.
